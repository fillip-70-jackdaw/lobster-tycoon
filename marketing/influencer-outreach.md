## Contact Strategy Notes

### For Creators (YouTubers/Streamers):
- **Best Approach:** Personal, casual outreach emphasizing unique angle
- **Email Format:** Find social media DMs first (more likely to be seen), then creator email if available
- **Subject Line:** Keep it short and personal: "Thought you'd dig this: Lobster Dealer Simulator"

### For Press:
- **Best Approach:** Professional press release format, but focused on the unique/newsworthy angle
- **Email Format:** Use official press contact if available, otherwise editor in charge of indie coverage
- **Subject Line:** Professional but interesting: "PRESS RELEASE: The World's First Lobster Dealer Simulation"

### What Works Best:
1. **Game Link First** — Let them try it immediately (browser, no setup)
2. **Unique Hook** — "World's first lobster dealer simulation" is genuinely unique
3. **Keep It Short** — Creators/press get hundreds of pitches. Respect their time.
4. **Mention Vanilla JS** — This resonates with the technical/indie community (built with no frameworks)
