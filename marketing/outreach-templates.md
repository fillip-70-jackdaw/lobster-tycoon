## Template Customization Guide

### For Creators — What to Personalize:

1. **[CREATOR NAME]** → Use their actual name or channel name
2. **[RECENT VIDEO]** → Reference a specific recent video they made
   - Example: "I loved your recent video on Satisfactory automation — the time management aspect"
   - Example: "Your coverage of Startup Company was perfect, and MLDT has similar vibes"
3. **Tone adjustment:** For streamers, emphasize replay value and interesting moments. For single-player channel creators, emphasize discovery and uniqueness.

### For Press — What to Customize:

1. **[DEREK EMAIL/CONTACT INFO]** → Your actual contact info (email, Twitter, Discord)
2. **[YOUR PUBLICATION]** → If this is a personalized press release, reference their site/recent coverage
   - Example for IndieDB: "Submitting to IndieDB's Indie Game Community"
   - Example for Rock Paper Shotgun: "Following your excellent coverage of browser-based indie titles"

### Genre Tags for Press Targeting:
- **For Tycoon/Simulation Press:** Emphasize the "freshness decay is unique" angle
- **For Indie Game Press:** Emphasize "solo developer, vanilla JS, genuine passion project"
- **For Business Game Press:** Emphasize "real economic decision-making under constraint"
- **For Tech/Browser Game Press:** Emphasize "no frameworks, no build system, pure vanilla JavaScript"

## Send Order Priority

**Start with:**
1. Template 1 to 5-10 small creators (under 50K)
2. Template 3 to 3-5 press outlets (IndieDB, GameDiscoverCo, The Indie Informer first)

**Then:**
3. Template 2 to 5-10 medium creators (50K-500K)

**Why:** Small creators will likely respond first and give early validation. Press notices will amplify reach. Medium creators are most likely to feature the game prominently.

## Post-Send Follow-Up

If you don't hear back after **5 days:**
- Try a different contact method (Twitter/Discord DM if they have it)
- Keep message SHORT: "Hey [NAME], just wanted to check if you got my earlier message about the lobster tycoon game. No pressure — would love your thoughts if interested!"

Never send more than 1 follow-up. Respect their time.
